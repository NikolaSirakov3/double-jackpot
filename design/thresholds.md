# Runtime thresholds

- Target: 60 FPS on desktop and a 390×844 mobile viewport.
- Device pixel ratio cap: 1.5.
- Spin input is ignored while a spin or wheel award is resolving.
- Reel stagger: 140 ms; total normal spin presentation: about 1.2 seconds.
- Balance starts at 1,000 credits; total bet options are 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20.
- The five-line paytable is scaled from the documented 5-credit total-bet baseline.
- All random outcomes use a seeded deterministic generator; presentation particles may not
  influence game outcomes.
